## M648 ???

_WBS INFO · Nov 1, 2006|39_

> what you want is an M207 (they are yellow) they convert 24V to phantom power

— **kuzub**, Nov 1, 2006|39

> Hi Steve, the M648 is a 48 volt supply used for phantom power in the larger consoles and to power up the M441 series modules. The M207 card will convert 24 to 48 volts but these are not very reliable and I would recommend just building a 48 volts supply with a $12 transformer and $3 LM317 regulator than the M207 doubler circuit. Out of 6 M207 cards I have only found 1 or 2 that work properly.
>
> Cheers, Dave Thomas
